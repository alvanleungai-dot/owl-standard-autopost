## D. LIVE-BEHAVIOR RISK FLAGS

All items below are owner-gated.  No reconciliation edit should be made while an affected scheduled task can read a half-reconciled manifest.

- **SCE-FB-Topup:** `scripts/fb-topup.mjs` invokes Better Life, TML, Sunmood, and Bright Day API schedulers.  Edits to all Better Life/TML/Bright Day `weeks/*.json`, their `fbPostId` fields, caption/poster paths, or status fields can cause the next top-up to schedule a post it considers unscheduled.  The Better Life duplicate-date decision is the highest risk because two distinct IDs are already recorded for each affected slot.
- **IG daily publishing:** Better Life and TML are IG-enabled.  Their week manifests are read by their IG publishers (including `scripts/tml/api-ig-daily.mjs`); an empty/removed `igPostId` for a due post can lead to a fresh Instagram publish.  Preserve the cross-post rule: no replacement record or new post is allowed on only Facebook.
- **Bright Day unattended scheduling:** `scripts/bright-day/weekly-run.mjs` is new and calls `gen-week.mjs` then `api-schedule.mjs`; `windows/bright-day-gen-week.cmd` is its scheduled-task entry point.  The proposed changes to D1/D2 generators, the new D3/D4 generators, `gen-week.mjs`, design contract, and Bright Day weeks therefore affect paid image generation and Facebook scheduling.
- **SCE-Catchup:** the modified `scripts/catchup.mjs` now includes Bright Day and can invoke its weekly runner during its Fri–Sun window.  This can create content and then schedule it, so it must not be enabled/registered until the Bright Day design/content state is owner-approved.
- **Gatekeeper and SCE-TaskHealth:** no audited drift changes their core scripts, but `SCE-TaskHealth` watches Bright Day task names and can repair task registration. - **Golf and authenticated setup:** `scripts/fb-derive-golftravel.mjs` and `scripts/copilot/fb-dress-page.mjs` touch Facebook-facing workflow; `scripts/copilot/notion-connect.mjs`, `warm-notion.mjs`, and `scripts/notion/*` are authenticated external integration surfaces.
